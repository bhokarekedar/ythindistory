Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how n8n YouTube Publisher ("the Application", "we", "us") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you connect and authenticate your Google / YouTube account.

1. Information We Access

The Application requests OAuth 2.0 access to the YouTube Data API exclusively to perform the following operations:

  • YouTube Upload (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload): Allows the Application to upload video files, set metadata (title, description, tags), and manage privacy status on your authorized YouTube channel.
  • Account Authentication: Basic profile identifiers (email address) necessary to verify the authenticated YouTube channel owner.

2. How Your Data Is Used

The data and credentials accessed by the Application are strictly used for:

  • Automating the publishing of synthesized video recaps directly to your personal YouTube channel as private/public uploads.
  • Attaching user-configured titles, descriptions, and tags to uploaded videos.
Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
The Application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Data Storage & Security

The Application is a self-hosted automation workflow hosted privately by the user. OAuth tokens and refresh tokens are stored locally within the user's encrypted self-hosted instance (e.g., n8n). No credentials, access tokens, video files, or user data are ever transmitted to or stored on external third-party servers.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data or Google credentials with any third parties under any circumstances.

5. Revoking Access

You can revoke the Application's access to your Google/YouTube account at any time by visiting your Google Security settings at Google Account Permissions.
